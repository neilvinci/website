Ch. 02 / CCommercial judgmentCustomer → Sales

Not every customer signal is an opportunity.

But some are.

I identify commercial signals in everyday customer conversations, then decide whether they belong with Sales. That means weighing business need, projected opportunity value, existing Sales engagement, and account context.

Sometimes an opportunity is already in motion with Sales, and the useful thing is connecting new customer-side information to it.

~US$5K Key benchmark

A guide, not a hard rule. Some legitimate opportunities sit below it. Some sit well above it.

  1. Customer conversation

    Usually a product or account question.

  2. Business need

    What the customer is actually trying to do.

  3. Commercial signal

    Growth, scope, or intent that goes beyond the question.

  4. Context

    Account history and existing Sales engagement.

  5. Qualification

    Projected value, fit, and whether AE involvement is warranted.

  6. Sales

    A handoff with context, or new information added to an opportunity already in motion.
