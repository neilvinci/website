Ch. 01 / CCommercial judgmentCustomer → Sales

Not every customer signal is an opportunity.

But some are.

I identify commercial needs in everyday customer conversations, then assess customer needs, projected value, and existing Sales engagement to decide whether Account Executive involvement makes sense.

Sometimes an opportunity is already in motion with Sales, and the useful thing is connecting new customer-side information to it. The opportunity itself stays with Sales.

US$5,000 Projected ARR

Generally required for AE involvement, with separate handling for existing sales-assisted accounts.

  1. Customer conversation

    Usually a product or account question.

  2. Business need

    What the customer is actually trying to do.

  3. Commercial signal

    Scope, growth, or intent that goes beyond the question.

  4. Context

    Account history and existing Sales engagement.

  5. Qualification

    Projected value, fit, and whether AE involvement is warranted.

  6. Sales

    Facilitated AE engagement, or new information added to an opportunity already in motion.
